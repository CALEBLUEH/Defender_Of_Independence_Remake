The untouched glTF JSON is retained as `scene.gltf.source` alongside `scene.bin` and the supplied `license.txt` in the `Source` folder. The archive suffix prevents Unity's glTF importer from redundantly importing the source after `Models/Dust2_Map.obj` has already been generated. The OBJ is a local, lossless geometry conversion made for Unity's built-in model importer. It preserves the 34 meshes, 12,408 vertices, 9,294 triangles, UV coordinates, normals, and material assignments. The textures are copied unchanged from the supplied package.
